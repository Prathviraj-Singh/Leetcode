# GCD of Two Numbers

**Difficulty:** Easy

## Problem Statement

Given two integers `a` and `b`, find their Greatest Common Divisor (GCD).

The GCD is the largest positive integer that divides both numbers without leaving a remainder.

## Example 1

**Input:**
```text
a = 12, b = 18
```

**Output:**
```text
6
```

**Explanation:** Common factors are 1, 2, 3 and 6. The greatest is 6.

## Example 2

**Input:**
```text
a = 24, b = 36
```

**Output:**
```text
12
```
